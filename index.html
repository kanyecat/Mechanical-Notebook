Generators

Design your own parts, check them for problems, and export files for laser cutting, CNC or 3D printing.

Gear generator

Spur gears, ring gears and racks with true involute teeth. Connect as many gears as you like into a train and watch them turn.

SVGDXFSTL
Open the gear generator →

Linkage generator

Four-bar and slider-crank linkages, or build your own multi-link mechanism and trace the path of any point.

SVGDXFSTL
Open the linkage generator →

Cam generator

Program a motion step by step, choose a follower, and check the pressure angle and undercut before you cut.

SVGDXFSTL
Open the cam generator →

Pulley generator

Place pulleys anywhere, join them with belts and hang loads on them, or rig a block and tackle. See speeds, grip and whether it can lift.

SVGDXFSTL
Open the pulley generator →

Chain and sprocket generator

Roller chain and sprockets in ANSI and ISO sizes. Place sprockets anywhere, run several chains, pick skeleton or swirl sprockets and cut every part.

SVGDXFSTL
Open the chain generator →
